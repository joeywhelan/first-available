# Summit Home Services: Service Guide

## Service visit fee
A technician visit costs **$89** for diagnosis. The fee is **waived** if you go ahead with the repair during the same visit. Parts are priced separately.

## Dishwasher error codes
These codes appear on Northfield, Kestrel, and Aspenware dishwashers.

| Code | Meaning | Try this first |
|---|---|---|
| **E15** | Water detected in the base (a leak). | Turn the dishwasher off, wait 30 minutes, and check the door seal for tears. |
| **E21** | The drain pump is blocked or not running. | Clean the filter at the bottom of the tub. |
| **E22** | The filter is clogged. | Remove the filter, rinse it under warm water, and reinstall it firmly. |
| **E24** | **Drain problem:** water isn't draining from the tub. Often a clogged filter, a kinked drain hose, or a failed drain pump. | Clean the filter and check that the drain hose under the sink isn't kinked. If water still sits in the bottom, book a technician. |
| **E25** | The drain pump is faulty or jammed. | Check the pump cover under the filter for broken glass or debris. |
| **E31** | The water inlet valve isn't letting water in. | Make sure the water supply valve under the sink is fully open. |

If the code comes back after trying the simple check, book a technician visit.

## Ovens and ranges
- **The oven won't heat:** check that the oven isn't in "Sabbath" or demo mode. Gas ovens with a weak igniter often click but don't light; that needs a technician.
- **It bakes unevenly:** a failing temperature sensor or heating element is the usual cause.

## Refrigerators
- **It isn't cooling:** make sure the vents inside aren't blocked, and that the condenser coils aren't covered in dust.
- **The ice maker has stopped:** check that the water filter isn't overdue (replace it every six months).

## Booking and parts
- Technicians are booked by ZIP code and appliance brand. Have your ZIP code, brand, and a short description of the problem ready.
- Replacement parts can be looked up by part number, model number, or a plain description.
