**Design principle:**
The image communicates a single idea: "the words find the gaskets; the model number picks one." Only the top card is outlined, and the amber model-number arrow must point at it.

**Text accuracy:**
Every string must be copied character-for-character; the only permitted strings in the frame are: `rubber strip around the door`, `search_parts`, `KS-GSK-412`, `fits KD-400 KD-412`, `NF-GSK-300`, `fits ND-300`, `KS-GSK-600`, `fits KD-600`, `KD-412`, `$24.50`, `in stock`. Underscores, camelCase and punctuation are intentional; do not add spaces, hyphens or line breaks inside a name. Pay particular attention to the letters and digits in each part and model number; `KS-GSK-412` appears twice by design. Proofread every label letter by letter before delivering; regenerate rather than deliver a misspelled frame.

**Exclusions:** No legend, caption box, title, footnote, colour key, or any explanatory text block. No logos, brand marks, icons of real products, or watermarks. No browser, terminal, or device mock-up. No photos or drawings of appliances, gaskets, wrenches or shopping carts. Only the nodes, arrows, and labels listed above appear in the frame.
